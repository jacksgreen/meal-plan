## Recipe Ideas

**Lentil Bolognese** - A few good options:
- [20 Minute Vegetarian Lentil Bolognese](https://www.asaucykitchen.com/20-minute-vegetarian-lentil-bolognese/) - Quick version
- [Kristine's Kitchen Lentil Bolognese](https://kristineskitchenblog.com/lentil-bolognese/) - Uses lentils + mushrooms
- Or look up Bon Appétit's black lentil version

Use green or brown lentils. Mushrooms add meatiness.
